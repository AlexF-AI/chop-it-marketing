# Chop it vs ChatGPT: What Each Does Better

*By [Alex Fahey](/author/alex-fahey), founder of Chop it. Last updated 5 August 2026. I built one of the two products being compared, so the format here is factual capability, not judgement. Where ChatGPT is better, it says so.*

The honest answer to "should I just use ChatGPT?" starts with an admission: ChatGPT creates Chop it's recipes. The two are not rivals. Chop it is GPT with guardrails, the staging, refinement and structure wrapped around the generation, and the question is whether those guardrails are worth having.

## The capability split

| Capability | ChatGPT alone | Chop it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invent a recipe from a request | Yes, best in class | Yes, GPT generates it |
| Adapt a recipe to constraints | Yes, best in class | Yes, through the same conversation |
| Staged refinement of the week | You prompt each round yourself | Built in, tuned for plant variety |
| UK phrasing, metric, UK methods | On request, and it drifts | Always |
| Recipe output | Prose in a chat window | Recipe card with macros and a diversity wheel |
| Remember your week on Thursday | No, it lives in a scrolled chat | Yes, the week is a persistent plan |
| One merged shopping list across recipes | On request, hit and miss | Yes, structured merge with totals |
| Know what is in your cupboards | No | Yes, including photographed shops |
| Keep recipes from cookbooks, websites and social | No | Yes, photo, link and social import |
| Find a recipe you cooked in March | Search a chat history | Search a structured library |
| Cost | Free tier is enough | Free to start; Pro £3.99 a month |
| Platforms | Everywhere | iPhone and inside ChatGPT |

## Where ChatGPT alone wins

Generation and adaptation, without qualification. Ask for a dinner from half a cabbage, three eggs and feta, and nothing matches it. Ask for the same dish cheaper, dairy-free or in one pan and it rewrites on the spot. If your entire need is ideas on demand, ChatGPT alone is the right answer and it costs nothing. Our guide to [getting a full week out of it with no app at all](/blog/chatgpt-meal-planning) covers many people's whole need.

It is also everywhere, on any device, which Chop it is not: the app is iPhone-only today.

## What the guardrails add

Since the same model writes the recipes either way, the difference is what happens around the generation.

**Staging.** A bare prompt gives you one answer shaped by whatever you thought to ask. Chop it stages the request and refines the result before it reaches you, and the staging is tuned: menus push plant count up, and the week's recipes keep fibre and protein high by design rather than by luck. You can ask ChatGPT for the same emphasis, but you have to ask, every time, and hold it there across a whole week of requests.

**UK phrasing and methods.** Coriander, not cilantro. Aubergine, not eggplant. Metric quantities, UK pack sizes, a grill that means a grill. ChatGPT does this when asked and drifts when the conversation runs long. Chop it enforces it on every recipe.

**The recipe card.** Generation ends as prose in a chat window. Chop it turns the same output into a structured card: ingredients with real quantities, a method you can cook from, macros, and a diversity wheel showing the plant variety across your week. The wheel is directional guidance on variety, nothing more, and that structure is what makes [the merged shopping list](/learn/how-ai-shopping-lists-work) possible at all.

## Where Chop it wins outright

Everything that requires memory. A conversation cannot hold state: the week you planned on Sunday is prose in a scrolled chat by Wednesday, the shopping list cannot be ticked off one-handed in a supermarket, and the model does not know you already own rice. These are not model weaknesses that better prompting fixes; they are what a conversation is.

Chop it holds the state: the week as a plan you open, the list as a structured merge checked against a pantry, the library as a searchable collection that also takes [recipes from cookbooks, websites and social video](/learn/how-to-save-recipes-from-anywhere), which is most of a real household's cooking and something ChatGPT does not attempt.

## Using them together

The intended pattern: plan in ChatGPT the way you already do, with [Chop it running inside it](https://chatgpt.com/plugins/plugin_asdk_app_6a1c0dfa60b88191b3360153a1c6613c). The conversation stays the conversation. What changes is that the results come back with the guardrails applied and become things: a week, a list, recipe cards you keep.

Current limits, stated rather than buried: signing in to your Chop it account inside ChatGPT is not live yet, so the handoff into the iPhone app is explicit. There is no Android app. AI features in the app run on credits, free to start, because the app pays for those AI calls itself.
