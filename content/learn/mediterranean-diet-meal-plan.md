# Mediterranean Diet Meal Plan for a UK Week: Four Dinners and One Shopping List

*By [Alex Fahey](/author/alex-fahey), founder of Chop it. Last updated 6 September 2026.*

**Short answer:** a Mediterranean week at home is four or five dinners built on olive oil, vegetables, beans and lentils, fish once or twice, chicken or eggs on the other nights, and cheese and yoghurt in supporting roles. You ban nothing and you swap nothing for a "healthy" imitation. Below is a real four-dinner week from the Chop it library, with the plant count and fibre for each recipe, followed by what the shopping list looks like and how to plan it in two minutes.

## What makes a week Mediterranean

The Mediterranean diet is a pattern of eating. The British Dietetic Association [describes it](https://www.bda.uk.com/resource/mediterranean-diet.html) as plenty of vegetables, fruit, beans, lentils, nuts and wholegrains; olive oil as the main fat; fish and seafood regularly; moderate poultry, eggs, cheese and yoghurt; and red meat and sweets only occasionally. The best-known trial of the pattern, PREDIMED, followed 7,447 adults in Spain and found fewer major cardiovascular events among those eating a Mediterranean diet with extra olive oil or nuts ([Estruch et al., 2018](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa1800389)).

For a family cooking dinner on a Tuesday, that translates into four habits:

1. Cook in olive oil and finish with it.
2. Put a vegetable or a tin of beans in every dinner, not on the side of it.
3. Eat fish at least once a week.
4. Let cheese and yoghurt season the plate rather than cover it.

Chop it's recipe rules already work this way. We write each recipe taste-first: add a plant or a pulse before swapping anything out, and keep the pasta, rice and cheese as they are. For the same reason we picked the [Mediterranean collection](/recipes/collection/mediterranean) by hand instead of filtering on the word "Greek".

## A four-dinner Mediterranean week

These four are from the Mediterranean collection. Plant counts are the number of different plant foods in the recipe, counted the same way the [Weekly Diversity Score](/features/weekly-diversity-score) counts them. Fibre is per serving, from each recipe's computed nutrition.

| Night | Dinner | Time | Plants | Fibre per serving |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | [Butter Bean Smash with Jammy Tomatoes & Feta](/recipes/butter-bean-smash-with-jammy-tomatoes-and-feta) | 30 min | 10 | 12.8 g |
| Tuesday | [Pan-Fried Sea Bass with Salsa Verde & Warm Lentils](/recipes/pan-fried-sea-bass-with-salsa-verde-and-warm-lentils) | 34 min | 13 | 13.1 g |
| Wednesday | [Chicken Thighs with Crispy Chickpeas & Herb Oil](/recipes/chicken-thighs-with-crispy-chickpeas-and-herb-oil) | 50 min | 9 | 7.0 g |
| Thursday | [Stuffed Romano Peppers with Rice, Herbs and Pine Nuts](/recipes/stuffed-romano-peppers-with-rice-herbs-and-pine-nuts) | 55 min | 13 | 8.7 g |

Two of the four are meat-free, one is fish, one is chicken. Three of the four use a tin or a pouch of pulses, which is where most of the fibre comes from. If you want a fifth night, [Crispy Halloumi with Herby Orzo](/recipes/crispy-halloumi-with-herby-orzo) is 24 minutes and ten plants.

The UK average fibre intake is about 20 g a day against a recommended 30 g ([NHS](https://www.nhs.uk/live-well/eat-well/digestive-health/how-to-get-more-fibre-into-your-diet/)). This week's dinners deliver between 7 g and 13 g per serving, which covers a quarter to nearly half of the daily target before you count breakfast and lunch.

## What the shopping list looks like

Merged across the four dinners, the list is short and repeats itself, which is the point of planning a week rather than four separate meals:

- **From the cupboard:** olive oil, tinned butter beans, tinned chickpeas, a pouch of puy lentils, long-grain rice, pine nuts, capers, red wine vinegar, Dijon mustard, dried currants, and the spice rack (cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, allspice, cinnamon, chilli flakes).
- **Fresh:** cherry tomatoes, Romano peppers, a courgette, spinach, rocket, garlic, onions and a shallot, lemons and a lime, flat-leaf parsley and mint.
- **Protein and dairy:** sea bass fillets, bone-in chicken thighs, a block of feta, natural yoghurt.

You will still have most of the cupboard line next week, so the second list is shorter than the first. In the app the list is generated from the plan and checked against your [pantry](/features/pantry), so the olive oil you bought on Monday does not reappear on Friday.

## How to plan it in two minutes

1. Open This Week in the [iPhone app](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/chop-it/id6762079343) or [Chop it in ChatGPT](/learn/chop-it-vs-chatgpt).
2. Tap **Pick 4 dinners for me**, or browse the Mediterranean collection and add the four above.
3. Set the week live. The shopping list and the [Weekly Diversity Score](/features/weekly-diversity-score) update as you swap dinners.
4. On Sunday the app reminds you to plan the next one, and shows how this week scored.

The Sunday step matters more than any single recipe. In Chop it's usage data, most people who set a week live come back to the app in later months, and most who never set one open the app once and stop.

## Common questions

**Is the Mediterranean diet good for weight loss?** The large studies of the pattern, PREDIMED included, measured heart health and mortality rather than weight, and the pattern sets no calorie target. Treat it as a way of eating you can keep up. If weight matters to you, the practical lever is the same as for any pattern: cook at home more often, and planning a week makes that likely.

**Do I need to give up bread, pasta or cheese?** No. Pasta, bread and cheese are everyday foods across the Mediterranean. The pattern is about proportions: more vegetables and pulses on the plate, olive oil as the fat, cheese as seasoning. Chop it does not swap your orzo for a cauliflower version.

**How many plants should a week have?** Treat [30 plants a week](/blog/how-to-eat-30-plants-a-week) as a prompt. The four dinners in this plan contain 28 different plant foods between them by our count on 6 September 2026, because herbs, spices, pulses, grains and nuts all count. Breakfasts and lunches take a normal week past 30 without trying.

**Is the Mediterranean diet expensive?** Tinned pulses, seasonal vegetables and olive oil are among the cheapest things in a British supermarket. Fish is the variable. One fish night a week keeps it manageable, and tinned tuna or sardines are the traditional budget route.
